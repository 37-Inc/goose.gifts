## Learning

Arm P becomes more useful than a conventional headline treatment when the line
names a real care contradiction and the scene makes that contradiction physical.
The barbershop does not work with an unrelated novelty, and `HIS BEARD NEEDS
WATERING` creates a click question about the kit and growing process. A single
localized source mismatch was worth the third and final generation call; no
further revision is authorized or needed.
