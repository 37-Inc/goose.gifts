## Concept 1 — Proposal forensics

- Audience / board: white-elephant shoppers, prank-gift savers, and people
  collecting ridiculous relationship gifts; `Funny White Elephant Gifts`.
- Hook: a candlelit restaurant proposal scene is photographed with romantic
  restraint, then the open ring box reveals the tiny trousered butt and is
  annotated like evidence from a proposal gone wrong.
- Pause reason: the linen, candlelight, and burgundy palette look like a polished
  relationship editorial before the object creates the double take.
- Click reason: the image states that the box farts when opened but leaves the
  viewer wanting the mechanism, sound, and prank context on the destination.
- Exact product feature: the product is an authentic-looking white ring box
  containing a molded butt instead of a ring and is triggered by opening.
- Swap test: replacing the product with an unrelated novelty would destroy the
  proposal, no-ring, and opening-triggered evidence story.
- Fidelity risk: the model could invent a ring, make the butt anatomical or
  life-sized, omit the dark-blue trousers/black shoes, or convert the compact
  box into jewelry packaging. Reject any of those changes.
- Arm fit: strong. Three sparse facts can point to the box, the molded figure,
  and the opening action without a card or reusable grid.
- Decision: generate.

## Concept 2 — Courtship field specimen

- Audience / board: prank-gift and offbeat-romance savers; `Funny White Elephant
  Gifts`.
- Hook: the small open box sits in damp clover and tiny white flowers like a
  newly documented courtship species, photographed as serious natural history.
- Pause reason: tactile morning light and macro greenery make a saveable nature
  image before the impossible proposal object registers.
- Click reason: the field observation that it calls when opened creates a
  specific curiosity gap about the sound prank and the handmade object.
- Exact product feature: the opening-triggered fart, no-ring reveal, tiny
  2.5-inch box, and strange trousered-butt occupant make the mock courtship
  observation possible.
- Swap test: an unrelated object would not support the courtship call, proposal
  camouflage, or no-ring behavior.
- Fidelity risk: greenery could obscure the white box or the model could turn
  the molded butt into a living creature. Keep the entire box and source-like
  figure plainly inspectable.
- Arm fit: strong. The annotations function as observations inside one macro
  scene, not as an infographic around a cutout.
- Decision: generate.

## Concept 3 — Jewelry appraisal plate

- Audience / board: design-object and prank-gift savers.
- Hook: a gemologist's lamp, loupe, and dark-green felt treat the prank as a
  precious object under appraisal.
- Pause reason: the material contrast would be handsome and immediately legible.
- Click reason: the appraisal could foreground the authentic box and no-ring
  reveal.
- Exact product feature: the genuine ring-box form and fake proposal premise.
- Swap test: a different small novelty could survive too much of the same
  luxury tabletop treatment.
- Fidelity risk: a top-down evidence plate plus labels would read as a reusable
  product card and would not make the opening-triggered sound function visible.
- Arm fit: weak because the composition trends toward a specimen grid rather
  than an observed scene.
- Decision: reject before generation.
