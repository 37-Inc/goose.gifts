2. Verify the selected products again close to release. Resolve changed facts,
   exact variants and public display eligibility without copying stale approvals.

   otherwise make no tested/owned claim.

   retailer check on the exact release, with affiliate disclosure and the
   approved domain/account configuration. Keep current prices off the static
