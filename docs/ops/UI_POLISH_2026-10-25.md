## Reference decisions

Reviewed the actual desktop pages on October 25 Pacific:

- [ThisIsWhyImBroke](https://www.thisiswhyimbroke.com/): its repeated retailer
  actions make the destination obvious; discovery links stay close to the grid.
  Borrow clear actions, not price claims, saves/accounts, visual density or copy.
- [Touch of Modern](https://www.touchofmodern.com/): consistent image geometry
  and product grouping establish a curated hierarchy. Borrow image discipline,
  not the large promotional hero, countdown, signup, cart or artwork.

## Direction and representative review

Keep white, three desktop/two mobile columns, the exact headline, warm-orange
accent and all 24 opening gifts. Quiet image boundaries establish consistent
geometry; title/description/action form one predictable card. Product links say
“View gift”; retailer buttons retain “Check price on Amazon/Etsy,” gain a small
external-link SVG and disclose the new tab accessibly. No price or stock is
implied. Licensed images, original copy and catalog inputs are untouched.

The representative gallery was built from the accepted read-only snapshot and
reviewed at desktop and 390px before the focused follow-up pass. Root approved
that direction. Follow-up work adds full-width card-title tap areas, aligned
title rows, keyboard focus after Clear, decorative search skeletons, explicit
empty/error recovery, readable mobile detail text and footer discovery links.
The shared card also carries these improvements into guides and Random.
Howie keeps its unannounced state and uses the same retailer treatment when an
episode exists; no selection or episode was invented. The existing 404 already
has a clear explanation and Browse gifts recovery, so it is retained.
