## Prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: typography refinement of an existing 1200x630 Goose Gifts social sharing card
Input image: Image 1 is the edit target. Preserve the entire existing composition: exact small goose illustration and scale, warm ivory paper texture, nearly black ink color, lowercase goose.gifts wordmark, thin warm-orange vertical rule, spacious left/right layout, margins, and all other visual details.
Primary request: Change only the typography and optical hierarchy of the statement “The internet’s least serious gift catalog.” The current very thin high-contrast fashion Didone serif feels fragile, precious, and slightly aloof. Replace it with a warm, authoritative contemporary book-jacket serif in a confident medium-to-semibold weight: lower stroke contrast, generous x-height, open counters, sturdy curves, beautifully drawn punctuation, and subtle humanist personality. It should feel like an excellent independent publisher, literary magazine, or beloved design bookstore—not a luxury fragrance campaign, wedding invitation, startup, or children’s brand.
Text (verbatim): “The internet’s least serious gift catalog.” Render it exactly with the curly apostrophe and final period, no misspellings, omissions, substitutions, or extra words.
Composition: retain the existing two-line headline footprint and left alignment, but tune line spacing and optical size so it reads instantly in a small social preview. Give “least serious” a confident visual presence without separately coloring or styling those words. Keep the headline subordinate to neither the goose nor the wordmark; it is the main message.
Constraints: typography-only revision. Do not change, redraw, move, enlarge, or restyle the goose. Do not change the goose.gifts wordmark. Do not move the orange rule. Do not change the background, colors, layout, aspect ratio, or add any object. No CTA, badge, button, shadow, gradient, border, watermark, or additional text. Do not use a thin Didone serif, bubbly rounded display font, geometric startup sans, handwriting, script, distressed type, or cartoon lettering.
```
